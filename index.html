The Pages

Mooligan

A companion for Magic: The Gathering. It keeps the score at your table, knows every card ever printed, reads the cards in front of you through the camera, and follows what is winning in tournaments this week.

Nothing here needs reading in order. Like any index, this one is for finding the one thing you came for.

Starting a game

Open Play. Choose how many are at the table, and the life each player begins with: twenty for most formats, forty for Commander.

Tap Start. The screen divides into one seat for each player, each turned to face whoever sits there, so the phone can lie flat in the middle of the table.

Keeping score

Tap the upper half of a seat to gain a life, the lower half to lose one. Press and hold to count faster.

A small tally beside the total shows what has changed in the last few moments, so a hit for seven reads as −7 before it settles.

Ending a game

When the game is decided, choose Rematch to reset every total and keep the same seats, or New Game to choose players and starting life again.

By set

Sets are listed newest first, each with its symbol and the date it was released.

Open a set to see its cards in collector-number order, as they would sit in a binder.

Card to card

Open any card, then swipe sideways to move to its neighbour in the list you came from. A whole set can be walked this way, one card at a time, like turning pages.

Searching

Type part of a card’s name; results gather as you type. Search looks across every set, so a single name leads to every printing of the card.

Narrowing a list

Filters narrow whatever list you are looking at. Choose by colour, card type, rarity, or a range of mana value.

Filters stack. Each one you add narrows the list further, and each stays until you take it away.

By format

Choose a format — Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper or Commander — to see only the cards legal in it.

Combine it with colour to ask a sharper question: every blue card legal in Modern, say.

Sorting, and starting over

Sort any list by name, mana value, price or release date.

The filters in use sit in a row above the results. Tap one to remove it, or Clear to begin again.

The card page

Everything about a card, in one place: its art, its rules text, whether it is legal in each format, and its rulings, newest first.

Printings

Most cards have been printed more than once. Printings lists every one, with its set and finish.

Choose one and the whole page follows it: the art, the collector number, the price.

Prices

Current prices are shown for each finish. Open a price to see its history, and tell a spike from a trend before you buy or trade.

Pull rates

How likely you are to open a card: the odds per pack for each printing, in each product it comes in and in each finish.

Information tiles

The card page is laid out in tiles: legality, prices, pull rates, usage. Tap any tile to open it at full size, then swipe to move between them.

Double-faced cards

Some cards have two faces. Turn the card over to read its other side; the page follows whichever face is showing.

Scanning a card

Point the camera at a card and hold it steady. Mooligan reads the card on the phone itself, and once it is recognised a copy floats up the screen.

Keep going. Several cards can be scanned one after another without stopping.

After a scan

Tap a scanned card to open it among its printings, showing the same face that was in front of the camera. A double-faced card can be turned over from here, as anywhere else.

What is winning

Choose a format to see its archetypes, ranked by their share of recent tournament results. The numbers are refreshed twice a day.

Decklists

Open an archetype to see the decklists behind it, each with the event it came from and how it finished. Any card in a list opens its own page.

Card usage

How widely a card is played: its share of decks in each format and, for Commander, how often it is chosen across the whole format.

Where to play

Find events at stores near you — Friday Night Magic, prereleases, Commander nights — and the premier play calendar of tournaments ahead.

Mooligan is open source, at github.com/missingems/Mooligan. With card data from Scryfall and MTGJSON, metagame from MTGGoldfish, Commander usage from EDHREC and the calendar from magic.gg.
