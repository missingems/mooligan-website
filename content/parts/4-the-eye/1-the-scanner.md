Point the camera at a card and hold it steady. Mooligan reads the card on the phone itself, and when it recognises the card, a copy floats up the screen.

> **For a clean read:** good light and a flat card. Sleeves are fine; glare is not.

![Framed, read, and lifted.](figure:scanner)

## After a scan
<!-- index: scanner; scanner > opening a scanned card; card page > opened from a scan; printings > of a scanned card; double-faced cards > scanning -->

Tap a scanned card to open it among its printings, showing the same face that was in front of the camera. From there it is an ordinary card page: turn it over, check its price, see where it is played.

## Several in a row
<!-- index: scanner; scanner > several cards in a row -->

Keep going. Cards can be scanned one after another without stopping, which is the quickest way through a stack of new pulls or a trade binder.
