Open a card and everything about it is on one page: the art, the rules text, whether it is legal in each format, and its [[rulings]], newest first.

![The card, and the tiles that tell you about it.](figure:card-page)

## Tiles
<!-- index: card page; card page > tiles on; tiles, on the card page -->

Below the card, the page is laid out in tiles: legality, prices, pull rates, usage. Tap any tile to open it at full size, then swipe to move between them without returning to the card.

## Double-faced cards
<!-- index: card page; double-faced cards; double-faced cards > turning over -->

Some cards have two faces and no back. Turn a [[double-faced card]] over to read its other side; the rest of the page follows whichever face is showing.
