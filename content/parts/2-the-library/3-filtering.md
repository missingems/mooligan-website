Filters narrow whatever list is in front of you: a set, or the results of a search. Choose by colour, by card type, by [[rarity]], or by a range of [[mana value]].

Filters stack. Each one you add narrows the list further, and each stays until you take it away.

![Filters stack: each narrows what the last one left.](figure:stacking-filters)

## By format
<!-- index: filtering; filtering > by format; formats; formats > legality in; Commander > legality in -->

Choose a [[format]] (Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper or Commander) to see only the cards legal in it. Combined with colour, it answers the kind of question deckbuilders ask: every blue instant legal in Modern, say.

## Sorting, and starting over
<!-- index: filtering; filtering > clearing; sorting; prices > sorting by -->

Sort any list by name, mana value, price or release date. The filters in use sit in a row above the results; tap one to remove it, or `Clear` to begin again.
