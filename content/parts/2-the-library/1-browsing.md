Every card belongs to a [[set]], and sets are where browsing begins. They are listed newest first, each with its symbol and the date it was released.

Open one to see its cards in [[collector number]] order, the way they would sit in a binder.

![A set, in the order it was numbered.](figure:binder)

## Card to card
<!-- index: browsing; browsing > card to card -->

Open any card, then swipe sideways to reach its neighbour in the list you came from. A whole set can be walked this way, one card after another, as you would turn the pages of a book.
