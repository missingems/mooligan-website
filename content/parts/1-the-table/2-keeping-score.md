Tap the upper half of your seat to gain a life, the lower half to lose one. Press and hold to count faster, which saves a good deal of tapping when something large connects.

![A seat, halved. Twenty, less a hit for seven.](figure:seat-halved)

## The tally
<!-- index: life totals > tally of changes; game, how to start a > keeping score in -->

Beside each total, a small tally keeps count of what has just changed. A hit for seven reads as −7 for a moment, so everyone can check the arithmetic before it settles into the total.

> Useful when two things happen at once and someone asks, *what was that?*
