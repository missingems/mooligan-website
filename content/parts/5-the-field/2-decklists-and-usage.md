Open an archetype to see the decklists behind it, each with the event it came from and how it finished. Any card in a list opens its own page.

## Usage
<!-- index: metagame; usage, card; Commander > card usage in -->

The same question, asked from the other direction. Open a card and see how widely it is played: its share of decks in each format and, for Commander, how often it is chosen across the whole format.
