What the marks around the app mean.

## In lists {#symbols-lists}
- Hourglass — a set that isn't out yet → {#upcoming-sets}
- Chevron › — tap for more
- Grey pill — a set's code, e.g. `TDM`
- Tick in a filled circle — picked

## Turning a card over {#symbols-turn}
- ⇄ two arrows — a transforming card: turns to its back
- ↶ curved arrow — a modal double-faced or two-sided card: turns over
- ↻ arrow round a corner — a flip card: turns upside down

## Rarity {#symbols-rarity}
- Black — common
- Silver — uncommon
- Gold — rare
- Orange-red — mythic rare

## Mana {#symbols-mana}
- W U B R G — white, blue, black, red, green mana
- C — colourless mana
- A number — that much mana of any kind
- X — a number you choose
- Two colours in one symbol — hybrid: pay either
- Phyrexian symbol — pay the colour, or 2 life instead
- The tap symbol — tap the card

## Prices {#symbols-finish}
- Regular — black, white in dark mode
- Foil — orange
- Etched — purple
- `—` — no price

## Legality {#symbols-legality}
- Green — `Legal`
- Grey — `Not Legal`
- Red — `Banned`
- Blue — `Restricted`
