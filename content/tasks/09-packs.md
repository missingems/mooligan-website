Open a booster of almost any set, with its real odds, and see what you'd pull.

## Getting ready {#pack-preparing}
Where: a set's details → `Open Play Booster`; a card's Pull Rate → `Open Pack` → {#set-info}, {#card-pull-rate}
- ✕ at the top left — close, at any point
- `Rolling the pack` — deciding what's in it
- `Getting the cards` — downloading their pictures first, so nothing pops in
- `Couldn't open that pack` — tap `Try Again`

## Tear it open {#pack-tear} [screen]
- Drag across the pack from right to left
- Let go too early and it closes again
- Every few seconds the pack nudges open, as a hint

## Reveal the cards {#pack-reveal} [screen]
- Drag or flick the top card away — it lands in its slot along the bottom
- Under the top card — its rarity, price, and how rare it is, e.g. `1 in 1,234`
- A sparkle — foil
- The best card comes last
- A strong buzz — a rare, mythic, foil, or card worth $5 or more
- `3 / 14` — how far through; beside it, the value so far
- Back arrow — put the last card back
- `Reveal All` — see the whole pack
- Tap a card — its page → {#card-page}

## The finished pack {#pack-summary} [screen]
- The set, and what the pack is worth
- Every card, best last; tap one → {#card-page}
- `Open Another` — the same booster again; or pull up the next pack from the bottom
- After two packs — the tally so far, e.g. `2 packs · 28 cards · $9.12`
- `Done` — close

## What's in a pack {#pack-contents}
- Play Booster — 14 cards; Draft Booster — 15; Collector Booster — 15
- Packs follow each booster's real odds; where they aren't known, standard odds
