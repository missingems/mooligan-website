New to Mooligan? Four things to try first.

## Find your way around {#tip-tabs} [screen]
Everything in Mooligan is one tap from the bar along the bottom: every set ever printed, a search for any card, the camera to scan cards, and your settings.
- `Browse` — every set, newest first → {#tip-whats-coming}
- `Search` — the magnifying glass; any card or set → {#tip-commander}
- `Scan` — point the camera at a card → {#tip-scan-price}
- `Settings` — how up to date your cards and prices are → {#ref-settings}
- `Collection` — coming soon

## Scan your first card {#tip-first-scan} [screen]
Grab any card from your pile. Mooligan recognises it from its picture, so there's nothing to type.
1. Tap `Scan`, and allow the camera.
2. Hold the card flat in the middle of the screen.
3. Hold still until it lifts off the picture.
4. Tap it for everything about it.
- More on what it's worth → {#tip-scan-price}

## Open your first pack {#tip-first-pack} [screen]
Crack a booster of almost any set, with the real odds, and without spending a thing.
1. Tap `Browse`, then a set.
2. Tap the set's name at the top.
3. Tap `Open Play Booster`.
4. Drag across the pack to tear it open.
- More on opening packs → {#tip-free-packs}

## Use it at the shop, even without signal {#tip-offline}
Card shops and tournament halls are notorious for bad reception. Once Mooligan has downloaded its cards, you can browse every set and every card without a connection.
- Works offline — sets, each set's cards, prices already downloaded
- Needs a connection — Search, filters, a scan's look-up, rulings
> Open the app once on Wi-Fi after installing it, so the cards download.
