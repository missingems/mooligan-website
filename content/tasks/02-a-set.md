## Cards
1. Tap a set. ![](figure:set-open)
2. Scroll its cards. ![](figure:set-grid)

> More load as you reach the end.

## Set details
1. Tap the name at the top. ![](figure:set-title)
2. Read its code, date and size. ![](figure:set-info)

## Search
1. Tap search at the bottom. ![](figure:set-search)
2. Type part of a card’s name. ![](figure:set-search-type)

> Card names only, not rules text.

## Colour
1. Tap the mana symbols. ![](figure:chip-colour)
2. Pick one colour or more. ![](figure:chip-colour-pick)

> Colour here is colour identity. `Colorless` adds cards with none.

## Type
1. Tap the type. ![](figure:chip-type)
2. Pick one type or more. ![](figure:chip-type-pick)

> Two types show cards of either. `All` clears them.

## Sort
1. Tap the order. ![](figure:chip-sort)
2. Pick what, then which way. ![](figure:chip-sort-pick)

> Dearest first: `Price`, then `Descending`.
