## Choose a pack
1. Tap the pack in a set. ![](figure:pack-button)
2. Pick a booster. ![](figure:pack-menu)

> Play and Collector Boosters from 2024; Draft and Collector from 2019; Draft before.

## Tear it open
1. Drag across it, right to left. ![](figure:pack-tear)

> Let go too early and it closes again.

## Deal the cards
1. Throw the top card aside. ![](figure:pack-deal)
2. Read its rarity, price and odds. ![](figure:pack-capsule)

> The best card comes last.

## Take one back
1. Tap the back arrow. ![](figure:pack-undo)

## Reveal all
1. Tap `Reveal All`. ![](figure:pack-reveal-all)

## Open another
1. Pull up the next pack. ![](figure:pack-another)

> Or tap `Open Another`. The total keeps adding up.
