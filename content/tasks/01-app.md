## Tabs {#tab-bar}
Where: every screen
- `Browse` — the sets, and each set's cards → {#browse}
- `Search` — cards and sets, by name and by Scryfall's keywords → {#search}
- `Scan` — the camera → {#scanner}
- `Collection` — coming soon → {#collection}
- `Settings` — what's on the phone, and how fresh → {#settings}
- Pick a tab again — it is as you left it; Scan starts afresh
- `Explore` — the latest metagame, planned → {#explore}

## iPhone {#app-iphone}
- Tabs along the bottom
- Screens stack: the sets, then a set, pushed
- A card opens over the tabs, zooming out of the card tapped → {#card-page}
- Portrait only

## iPad {#app-ipad}
- Tabs along the top, as in Files and Music
- Browse in two columns: the sets beside the open set → {#browse-columns}
- `Hide Sets` / `Show Sets` — the button at the start of the bar, ⌃⌘S
- Search is a page of its own, its field under the tabs → {#search}
- A card opens over the tabs in columns → {#card-columns}
- Any orientation

## iPhone Duo {#app-duo}
- Closed: like an iPhone, the bars and tabs in a column at the side
- Open: like an iPad, in a split view
- Half open, as a book: the sets grow to the fold, cards keep either side of it
- Folding or unfolding keeps your place: tab, set, card, text typed

## Keyboard {#keyboard}
- ⌘F — Search, with its field ready → {#search}
- ⌃⌘S — hide or show the sets, iPad
- ↑ ↓ — move through the sets → {#sets-keyboard}
- → ← — between the sets and the grid
- Arrows, then Return or Space — pick a card in a grid → {#set-grid-keyboard}
- Escape — back, or close a card screen

## On launch {#launch}
- The card catalog comes with the app, unpacked the first time
- In the background: the catalog, then pack odds, then prices, then the scanner's images → {#settings}
- On iPad, the newest released set opens beside the sets
