Everything on and behind a card, explained.

## Tell printings apart {#tip-printings} [screen]
The same card can come in a dozen versions. The collector number tells them apart, and names special treatments like borderless or showcase.
1. Open a card.
2. Tap the `#` tile under it.
3. Read `Treatment` for special versions: Borderless, Showcase, Extended Art, Full Art, Etched, Serialized.
4. Scroll down to `Prints` for every other version, each with its price.

## Check whether it's a reprint {#tip-reprint} [screen]
First printings and reprints can be worth very different amounts.
1. Open a card.
2. Tap the set tile under it.
3. Read `Printing`: `First printing` or `Reprint`.

## See where a card's colours come from {#tip-identity} [screen]
Colour identity isn't just the mana cost: symbols in the rules text and basic land types count too. Mooligan lights up every one.
1. Open a card.
2. Tap the colour tile under it.
3. Tap a lit spot to see which colour it adds.
> In Commander, a card must fit within your commander's colour identity.

## Check it's legal in your format {#tip-legality} [screen]
Before you sleeve it up, check it's allowed.
1. Open a card and scroll to `Legality`.
2. Find your format among the ten.
3. `Legal` green, `Not Legal` grey, `Banned` red, `Restricted` blue: one copy only.

## Settle a rules argument {#tip-rulings} [screen]
Official rulings, dated, right on the card. No more searching mid-game.
1. Open a card and scroll to the bottom.
2. Tap `Rulings`.

## See the other side of a card {#tip-turn-over} [screen]
Double-faced cards are hard to read once they're sleeved. Turn them over on screen instead.
1. Open the card.
2. Tap the round arrow on its edge, or `Flip`, `Transform` or `Turn Over` under it.
3. The text, stats and tiles change to that side.

## Work out mana value with X {#tip-mana-value} [screen]
X spells, hybrid and Phyrexian mana make the total tricky. Let Mooligan count it out.
1. Open a card.
2. Tap the mana value tile.
3. Each symbol is counted out, with the total at the bottom.
> X counts as zero, except while the spell is being cast.

## Flip through a whole set, fast {#tip-strip} [screen]
Skim a set like a binder, without swiping card by card.
1. Open any card in a set.
2. Hold the strip of small cards at the bottom, and slide.
3. Let go on the card you want.
