## Scan
1. Tap the camera on `Sets`. ![](figure:scan-camera)
2. Hold one card in view. ![](figure:scan-point)
3. Hold still until it lifts. ![](figure:scan-lift)

✓ Even light. ![](figure:scan-light)
✗ Glare. ![](figure:scan-glare)

## Printings
1. Swipe to your printing. ![](figure:scan-printings)

> Reprints with the same art look alike to the camera.

## Double-faced cards
1. Tap the arrow to turn it. ![](figure:card-flip)

> Either face scans.

## Card page
1. Tap the card. ![](figure:scan-tap)

> Swipe between its printings there too.

## Next card
1. Tap the right-hand ✕. ![](figure:scan-next)

> The left-hand ✕ closes the scanner.
