## Card after card
1. Tap a card in a set. ![](figure:card-open)
2. Swipe for the next. ![](figure:card-swipe)

> Cards come in the set’s order, with its filters.

## The strip
1. Tap a card in the strip. ![](figure:strip-tap)
2. Or hold and slide along it. ![](figure:strip-scrub)

> You feel a tick for each card you pass.

## Turn it over
1. Tap the arrow on its edge. ![](figure:card-flip)

> Or `Flip`, `Transform` or `Turn Over` under the tiles. The text turns with it.

## Tilt
1. Tilt your phone. ![](figure:card-tilt)

> Printings made only in foil shimmer. Reduce Motion keeps the card still.

## Text
1. Read the name, cost and rules. ![](figure:card-text)

> Underlined words are keywords; italics in brackets, reminder text.

## Legality
1. Scroll to `Legality`. ![](figure:legality)

> Ten formats, each `Legal`, `Not Legal`, `Banned` or `Restricted`.

## Prints
1. Scroll to `Prints`. ![](figure:prints-row)

> Every other paper printing, in English.

## Tokens and pieces
1. Scroll past `Prints`. ![](figure:related-row)

> `Tokens`, `Combo Pieces`, `Meld Pieces` and `Meld Result`, where a card has them.

## Rulings
1. Tap `Rulings` at the bottom. ![](figure:rulings-tap)
2. Read each dated ruling. ![](figure:rulings-sheet)

> `Done` puts them away.
