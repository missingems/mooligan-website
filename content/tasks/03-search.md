Search that speaks Magic: by colour, cost, type, rules text and format.

## Find every card your commander can play {#tip-commander} [screen]
Building Commander means every card has to fit your commander's colour identity. Pick its colours and Mooligan shows only cards you're allowed to play.
1. Tap `Search`, then `Identity` above the keyboard.
2. Tap your commander's colours.
3. Tap C as well, to include colourless cards such as artifacts.
4. Tap Tab, then Return.
> Add `Type` creature, or a `Mana Value`, to narrow it down.

## Fill the gaps in your curve {#tip-curve} [screen]
Short on two-drops? Ask for exactly the cost you need.
1. Tap `Search`, then `Mana Value`.
2. Tap `≤`, then `2`, then Tab.
3. Add your colours with `Color`, then press Return.
> `≤` is at most, `≥` at least, `=` exactly.

## Find cards that do what you need {#tip-rules-text} [screen]
Need card draw, removal, or anything that makes treasure? Search the words on the card, not its name.
1. Tap `Search`, then `Rules Text`.
2. Type the words, e.g. draw a card.
3. Tap Tab, then Return.

## Check it's legal before you build {#tip-format-search} [screen]
Nothing hurts like building around a card you can't play. Add a format to any search and only legal cards come back.
1. Tap `Search`, then `Format`.
2. Pick your format, e.g. modern.
3. Add anything else, then press Return.

## Find one exact printing {#tip-printing-search} [screen]
After the version from a particular set? Type the set and the card together.
1. Tap `Search`.
2. Type the set's code and the card's name, e.g. `fra swamp`.
3. Tap `swamp in …` at the top.
4. Tap the card's name: just that set's printings.

## See every mythic in a set {#tip-mythics} [screen]
Know what you're chasing before you open a pack.
1. Tap `Search` and type `in:` with the set's code, e.g. `in:fin`.
2. Pick the set.
3. Tap `Rarity`, then `mythic`.
4. Press Return.

## Sort a set by colour and type {#tip-set-filters} [screen]
Looking at one set? Filter it right on its page, no searching needed.
1. Open a set in `Browse`.
2. Tap the colour button and pick colours.
3. Tap the type button and pick types, e.g. `Creature`.
> Pick `Colorless` with your colours to keep artifacts and lands in.
