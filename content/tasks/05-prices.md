## Market price
1. Scroll to `Market Price`. ![](figure:prices-row)
2. Tap a price for TCGplayer. ![](figure:prices-tap)

> In US dollars, for each finish: `Regular`, `Foil`, `Etched`.

## Price history
1. Hold the chart a moment. ![](figure:chart-hold)
2. Slide to a day. ![](figure:chart-scrub)

> Up to 90 days. Set symbols mark new releases.

## Buy back
1. Tap `Buy Back`. ![](figure:buyback-tap)
2. See what Card Kingdom pays. ![](figure:buyback-sheet)

> The share is its buying price against its own selling price.
