## Format {#metagame-format} [planned]
- Modern, Standard, Pioneer, Legacy, Pauper, Vintage, Premodern, Penny Dreadful, Duel Commander
- Pick one — the page follows it
- Kept between visits — to be decided

## Time frame {#metagame-window} [planned]
- `7 days`, `14 days`, `30 days` — 30 by default; Duel Commander has 30 only
- Kept between visits — to be decided
- Updated twice a day

## Popular decks {#popular-decks} [planned]
- Colour share → {#colour-share}
- Every archetype of the format, by share → {#archetype-row}
- Tap an archetype → {#archetype}

### Colour share {#colour-share} [planned]
- White, Blue, Black, Red, Green, Colourless — each a percentage of the decks, together 100%
- Weighted by each archetype's share and the colours its decks ask for

### Archetype row {#archetype-row} [planned]
Where: Explore → latest meta; Metagame → popular decks
- Keycards — usually two, the archetype's most important cards
- Colours — the colours the deck plays
- Name — colours or guild, then the archetype, card or combo, e.g. Mono-Black Necro
- Share — % of the format's decks in the time frame
- Top 8s — results that finished in the top 8 in the time frame
- Wins — first places in the time frame
- Trend — up or down against a longer time frame
- Tap it → {#archetype}

## Recent events {#recent-events} [planned]
- The newest 30 events, Leagues too
- Filter by kind: Pro Tour, Regional Championship, RCQ, Store Championship, MTGO Challenge, MTGO League
- Each an event row → {#event-row}

### Event row {#event-row} [planned]
Where: Explore → latest event; Metagame → recent events
- Name, date, kind, number of decks
- Winner — archetype and player; none for a League
- The top three — finish, archetype, player, colours, keycards
- Tap it → {#event}

- All, Creature, Planeswalker, Instant, Sorcery, Artifact, Enchantment, Battle, Land, Sideboard
- Each card — decks playing it, share, average copies, trend
- Basic lands left out
- Tap a card → {#card-page}
