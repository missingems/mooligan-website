What tournament players are playing, in every format, updated twice a day.

## The page {#metagame-page} [screen]
Where: Explore → `View more`, or the whole metagame → {#explore}
- A format and a time frame at the top; colours, decks, events and cards below

## Choose a format {#metagame-format}
- Modern, Standard, Pioneer, Legacy, Pauper, Vintage, Premodern, Penny Dreadful, Duel Commander
- Tap one — the whole page follows it

## Choose a time frame {#metagame-window}
- The last `7 days`, `14 days` or `30 days`; 30 to begin with
- Duel Commander has the last 30 days only

## Popular decks {#popular-decks}
- Colour share at the top → {#colour-share}
- Every archetype in the format, most played first → {#archetype-row}
- Tap an archetype → {#archetype}

### Colour share {#colour-share}
- White, Blue, Black, Red, Green and Colourless — each colour's share of the decks played; together they make 100%
- A colour counts by how much of it the decks' mana asks for

### Reading an archetype row {#archetype-row}
Where: Explore → Latest meta; Metagame → Popular decks
- Keycards — usually two: the cards that define the deck
- Colours — the colours the deck plays
- Name — its colours or guild first, then the deck: a key card, a plan or a combo, e.g. Mono-Black Necrodominance, Boros Energy
- Share — of every deck in the format and time frame, how many are this archetype, e.g. 12.4%
- Top 8s — how many times it finished in a tournament's top 8 in the time frame
- Wins — how many tournaments it won
- Trend — up or down: playing more or less than over a longer time
- Tap the row → {#archetype}

## Recent events {#recent-events}
- The latest 30 tournaments, newest first, online and in person
- Filter by kind — Pro Tour, Regional Championship, RCQ, Store Championship, MTGO Challenge, MTGO League
- Tap an event → {#event}

### Reading an event row {#event-row}
Where: Explore → Latest event; Metagame → Recent events
- Name and date
- Kind — e.g. MTGO Challenge
- Number of decks that played
- Winner — the winning archetype and player; Leagues have no single winner
- The top three — each with its archetype, player, colours and keycards
- Tap the row → {#event}

## Popular cards {#popular-cards}
- The cards played most in the format, over the last 7, 14 or 30 days
- All cards, or by kind — Creature, Planeswalker, Instant, Sorcery, Artifact, Enchantment, Battle, Land — and Sideboard
- Each card — how many decks play it, their share, the average number of copies, and whether it's rising or falling
- Basic lands left out
- Tap a card → {#card-page}

## An archetype {#archetype} [screen]
Where: an archetype row in Explore or the Metagame, or an archetype in an event's standings
- Its name, colours and keycards
- Its share over the last 7, 14 and 30 days
- Recent decks — the latest 60 results, newest first: date, event, finish and player
- Tap a deck — its full list → {#decklist}
- Tap an event → {#event}
- Featured list — a typical list for the archetype → {#decklist}

## An event {#event} [screen]
Where: Explore → Latest event; Metagame → Recent events; a result under an archetype
- Name, date, kind and format
- The standings, in finishing order — finish, player, archetype, colours and keycards
- MTGO Leagues — every listed deck went 5–0, in no particular order
- Tap a deck → {#decklist}
- Tap an archetype → {#archetype}

## A decklist {#decklist} [screen]
Where: a deck in an archetype's results or an event's standings
- The archetype, the player, the format and the event
- `Mainboard` and `Sideboard` — how many copies of each card
- Tap a card → {#card-page}
- Copy the list — to paste into another app
