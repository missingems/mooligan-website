Where: Browse → a set; Explore → a set; Search → a set; a card's `View` set → {#browse}, {#search}, {#insight-set}
- Back — to the sets, iPhone
- Pull down to refresh

## Set details {#set-info}
Where: tap the set's name at the top; on iPad, its symbol at the end of the bar
- `Set Symbol`
- `Set Code` — e.g. `TDM`
- `Release Date` — e.g. `2025-04-11`
- `Number of Cards` — e.g. `286`
- `Open Play Booster`, `Open Draft Booster`, `Open Collector Booster` — sets sold in packs → {#pack-opening}

## Filters {#set-filters}
- iPhone: three chips over the grid, staying as it scrolls
- iPad: `Colour`, `Card Type` and `Sort` at the end of the bar
- Each opens a list; a picked row has a tick

### Colour {#set-filter-colour}
- `White`, `Blue`, `Black`, `Red`, `Green`, `Colorless`
- Pick several — cards whose colour identity fits within them
- `Colorless` with colours — adds colourless cards
- The chip shows the colours picked

### Card type {#set-filter-type}
- `All`, `Land`, `Artifact`, `Enchantment`, `Instant`, `Sorcery`, `Planeswalker`, `Creature`
- Pick several — cards of any of them
- `All` clears the rest

### Sort {#set-filter-sort}
- `Name`, `Price`, `Mana Value`, `Color`, `Rarity`, `Date`
- `Ascending`, `Descending`
- `Name`, `Ascending` at first

## Card grid {#set-grid}
- Card images, two to a row on iPhone, three or four on iPad
- More load as you near the end
- Turn button on double-faced cards — turn it over in place
- Tap a card → {#card-page}

### Keyboard {#set-grid-keyboard}
- Arrows — move a ring from card to card
- Return or Space — open the ringed card → {#card-page}
- ← from the first column — back to the sets, iPad
- Escape — back to the sets, iPhone

## Search within the set {#set-search}
- From Search: `in:` and the set → {#search-field}
- On iPad, a set opened from a card has its own field: `Search 286 cards…`

## When it fails {#set-states}
- A spinner while loading
- `Failed to find "<set>"` with `{T}: Try Again`
- `No results found.` — nothing matches the filters
