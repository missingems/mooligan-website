New sets, previews, errata and bans.

## See what's coming next {#tip-whats-coming} [screen]
Know when the next set lands. Upcoming sets sit at the top of Browse, with their release dates.
1. Tap `Browse`.
2. Look for the hourglass by the date: the set isn't out yet.

## Preview a set before it's out {#tip-preview} [screen]
See the cards revealed so far, and sort them by colour, type or rarity like any set.
1. Tap an upcoming set in `Browse`.
2. Use the colour, type and order buttons to sort them.

## Catch errata and bans {#tip-changes} [screen]
When a card's text, stats or legality changes, Mooligan notices. See exactly which cards changed, and when.
1. Tap `Settings` and scroll to `Activity`.
2. Tap a day.
3. Read the cards under `New`, `Changed` and `Removed`.

## Know your cards are up to date {#tip-fresh} [screen]
Cards are checked daily and prices four times a day, all by themselves. Settings shows when each last updated.
1. Tap `Settings`.
2. A green tick means up to date.
