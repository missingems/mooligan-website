## Card after card {#card-pager}
Where: iPhone; a closed Duo; Scan; a pack
- Swipe sideways — the cards it was opened among
- `Add to Collection` — the plus in the bar, not working yet

### The strip {#card-strip}
- A thumbnail per card, a lens on the one on show
- Tap a thumbnail — fly to that card
- Hold and slide — a card floats up; a tick for each one passed
- Flick — it coasts to a card

## Columns {#card-columns}
Where: iPad; an open Duo
- The list of cards — tap one, or use the arrows
- The card's page beside it
- Its decks beside that, or `Decks` in the bar → {#card-decks}
- Grabber or `Close` — close it

## The card {#card-image}
- Leans as you tilt the phone; printings only in foil shimmer
- Turn button on its edge — turn it over; the text, tiles and backdrop follow
- Name and mana cost
- Type line
- Rules text — keywords underlined, reminder text in italics
- Flavour text
- Split and adventure cards show both halves

## Information {#card-information}
- `Set & rarity` — symbol and code; the colour is the rarity → {#insight-set}
- `Collector Number` → {#insight-collector}
- `Color Identity` → {#insight-identity}
- `Mana Value` → {#insight-mana}
- `Loyalty Counters` — planeswalkers → {#insight-loyalty}
- `Power Toughness` — creatures → {#insight-pt}
- Tap a tile — its page; swipe for the next; pull down to close

### Set & Rarity {#insight-set}
- The card is a rarity from a set
- Common, uncommon, rare, mythic rare — how often a Play Booster holds one
- `Set`, `Code`, `Released`, `Set type`, `Printing`
- `View <set>` → {#set-page}

### Collector Number {#insight-collector}
- What the number tells: this printing, among the set's versions
- `Number`, `Set`, `Cards in set`, `Treatment` — borderless, showcase, extended art…

### Color Identity {#insight-identity}
- The card dimmed but for lit spots that give it colour
- Tap a spot — its colour
- A row per colour
- `Not printed as a symbol` — basic land types

### Mana Value {#insight-mana}
- Each symbol counted: generic, colours, hybrid, Phyrexian, X
- The total: `Mana value`

### Loyalty {#insight-loyalty}
- `Starting loyalty`
- `Abilities` — e.g. +1, −3, −8

### Power & Toughness {#insight-pt}
- `Power`, `Toughness`
- `In combat` — damage dealt, damage to destroy it

## Turn over {#card-turn-over}
- `Flip`, `Transform` or `Turn Over` — two-sided cards only

## Legality {#card-legality}
- The printing's release date
- Ten formats: Standard, Historic, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Pauper, Vintage, Penny, Commander, Brawl
- `Legal`, `Not Legal`, `Banned`, `Restricted`

## Pull Rate {#card-pull-rate}
- `Available in <n> Products`
- A pouch per booster — `Play Booster`, `Collector Booster`…
- `Regular`, `Foil`, `Any Finish` — `1 in 168 packs`, or `Every pack`
- `Open Pack` → {#pack-opening}
- Left out for sets never sold in packs

## Market Price {#card-market-price}
- `Past 3 Months` — the chart, a line per finish
- Set symbols on the chart — new sets coming out
- Hold, then slide — the prices on that day
- `Regular`, `Foil`, `Etched` — today's price; tap for TCGplayer
- `Buy Back` → {#buy-back}
- `No data available`; `Couldn't load prices` with `Retry`

### Buy Back {#buy-back}
- `Source` — `Card Kingdom`
- A row per finish — what it pays, and that as a share of its retail price
- `Sell to Card Kingdom`

## Prints and related {#card-prints}
- `Prints` — every other paper printing
- `Tokens`, `Combo Pieces`, `Meld Pieces`, `Meld Result`
- Each card with its set and price
- iPad: a grid of nine; tap a card → {#card-page}
- `View All` — every one, iPad

## Decks {#card-decks}
Where: a card in columns, iPad
- `Updated <when>`
- A block per format: `Played in 12% · 300 of 2,500 decks`
- Archetype rows — name, `2.3 copies on average`, `<n> decks` → {#archetype}
- `Commander` — `Included in 4% · …`, commanders that play it
- `Commander data from EDHREC`
- `No Decks`; `Decks Unavailable` with `Try Again`

## Related {#card-related}
- `Artist` — the artist's name
- `Rulings` — dated rulings, `Done` to close
