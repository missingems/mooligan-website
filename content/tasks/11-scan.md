Where: the `Scan` tab → {#tab-bar}
- Asks for the camera the first time
- Leave the tab — the camera stops

## Scanning {#scan-camera}
- `Loading...`, then `Scanning...`
- Hold one card in view — yellow corners lock onto it
- `Scan Found!`, then the card's name

## The result {#scan-result}
- The card lifts off the camera's picture and stands upright
- Its printings beside it, newest first — set, code, number, prices
- Swipe to your printing
- Either face of a double-faced card scans
- Tap a card → {#card-page}
- `X` at the top right — scan the next card
