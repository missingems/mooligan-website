Point the camera at a card and Mooligan finds it, with its printings and prices.

## Scanning {#scan-camera} [screen]
Where: the `Scan` tab → {#tab-bar}
- The first time, allow Mooligan to use the camera
- Leave the tab — the camera turns off
- `Loading...` — getting ready; `Scanning...` — ready
- Hold one card flat in the middle of the screen, filling much of it
- Yellow corners lock onto the card — hold still
- `Scan Found!`, then the card's name at the top

### For a good scan {#scan-tips}
- One card at a time
- All four corners in view
- Even light, without glare
- Hold still for a moment

## The result {#scan-result} [screen]
- The card lifts off the camera picture and stands upright; tilt your phone and it leans
- Under it — set name, symbol, code, collector number and prices
- Swipe sideways — the card's other printings, newest first
- Reprints with the same art look the same to the camera — swipe to yours
- Two-faced cards — either face scans; the round arrow turns it over → {#symbols-turn}
- Tap a card — its page; swipe between its printings there → {#card-page}
- ✕ at the top right — scan the next card
