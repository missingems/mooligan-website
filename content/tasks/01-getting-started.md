## The tabs {#tab-bar} [screen]
Along the bottom of the screen, on every page.
- `Browse` — every set, and the cards in each → {#browse}
- `Collection` — your own cards; coming soon → {#collection}
- `Settings` — what's on your phone, and how up to date it is → {#settings}
- `Scan` — point the camera at a card to look it up → {#scanner}
- `Search` — the magnifying glass at the end of the bar, for any card or set → {#search}
- Tap a tab — it opens just as you left it
- `Scan` and `Settings` start afresh each time you open them

## Moving around {#gestures}
- Tap — open a set, a card, a tile or a button
- Swipe sideways — to the next card, or the next page
- Swipe down — close a card, or a sheet that slid up
- Pull a list down — refresh it
- Hold, then slide — read a price on a chart, or skim through cards on the strip
- Back — top left, or swipe in from the left edge of the screen
- Tap outside a pop-up list — close it

## The first time you open it {#first-launch}
- The card catalog comes with the app, and is unpacked the first time you open it
- Newer cards, prices, pack odds and the scanner's card pictures then download by themselves → {#settings}
- The first time you scan, your iPhone asks to let Mooligan use the camera → {#scanner}

## Without a connection {#offline}
- The sets list — opens once it has loaded before
- A set's cards — open once the catalog has downloaded
- Filters, Search, rulings and a scan's look-up — need a connection
- Price history — shows the last prices downloaded
- Packs — need a connection for their cards' pictures
