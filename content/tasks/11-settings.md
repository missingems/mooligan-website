What's on your phone, and how up to date it is. Everything updates by itself.

## The page {#settings-page} [screen]
Where: the `Settings` tab → {#tab-bar}
- Nothing to set here
- Pull down — check again
- Each section — a mark, its name, its state and when it last ran
- Under each — the steps of its last run, and how long each took

## Marks {#settings-marks}
- Green ✓ — up to date
- Orange — running now
- Red ✕ — failed; it tries again later
- A grey circle with a line — a step that was skipped

## Card catalog {#settings-catalog}
- `<n> cards` — every card Mooligan knows
- `Up to date`, or `Not downloaded yet`
- `Came with the app` — still the catalog that came with the app
- Checked once a day; downloaded again at most once a week

## Activity {#settings-activity}
- A square for each day of the last six months; the darker, the more cards changed
- Each update — its date, and `+12` new, `~40` changed, `−1` removed
- Tap an update — its cards, under `New`, `Changed` and `Removed`
- A card counts as changed when its rules text, stats or legality change; new prices don't count

## Card scanner {#settings-scanner}
- `<n> cards` — the cards the scanner can recognise; `Ready`
- Brought up to date after the catalog → {#scanner}

## Pack odds {#settings-pack-odds}
- `Pack odds` — how many boosters have their odds; `Pull rates` — how many sets
- Checked once a day → {#card-pull-rate}

## Prices {#settings-prices}
- `Prices to <date>` — the latest day with prices
- Checked four times a day → {#card-market-price}
