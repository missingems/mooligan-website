## Sets won't load {#help-sets}
- Check your connection, then tap `Retry` → {#sets-failed}
- Pull the list down to try again

## A set's cards won't load {#help-set}
- Tap the `Try Again` button → {#set-failed}
- `No results found.` — the filters leave nothing; change them → {#set-filters}

## The camera stays black {#help-camera}
- Allow camera access: iPhone Settings → Mooligan → Camera

## The scanner can't find a card {#help-scan}
- One card, all four corners in view, even light → {#scan-tips}
- Hold still for a moment
- Tap ✕ at the top right, and try again

## The scanner found the wrong printing {#help-printing}
- Swipe sideways to yours: reprints with the same art look alike → {#scan-result}

## Prices won't load {#help-prices}
- Tap `Retry` → {#price-failed}

## A pack won't open {#help-pack}
- Tap `Try Again` → {#pack-preparing}

## Is my data up to date? {#help-fresh}
- Look in Settings → {#settings}
