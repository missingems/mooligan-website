## Up to date
1. Tap ≡, then `Settings`. ![](figure:menu-settings)
2. Look for the ticks. ![](figure:settings-status)

> Cards are checked daily, prices four times a day.

## Activity
1. Scroll to `Activity`. ![](figure:activity-heatmap)
2. Tap a day for its changes. ![](figure:activity-day)

> Changes to rules text, stats and legality count; new prices don’t.
