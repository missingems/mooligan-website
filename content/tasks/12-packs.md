Where: a set's details → `Open … Booster`; a card's Pull Rate → `Open Pack` → {#set-info}, {#card-pull-rate}
- Play Boosters from 2024; Draft before; Collector from 2019
- `X` — close

## Getting ready {#pack-preparing}
- `Rolling the pack`, then `Getting the cards`
- `Couldn't open that pack` with `Try Again`

## Tearing {#pack-tear}
- Drag across the pack, right to left
- Let go early and it closes again

## Revealing {#pack-reveal}
- `3 / 14` and the value so far
- Throw the top card aside — it lands in its slot
- Under it: rarity, price, `1 in <n>`
- Back arrow — take one back
- `Reveal All`
- Tap a card → {#card-page}

## The finished pack {#pack-summary}
- The set and the pack's value
- `3 packs · 42 cards · $18.40` — after a second pack
- Every card, best last → {#card-page}
- `Open Another`, or pull up the next pack
- `Done`
