- Pull down to refresh
- Each section: ✓ up to date, running, or ✕ failed, and when it last ran
- Each step of the last run, and how long it took

## Card catalog {#settings-catalog}
- `<n> cards` — `Up to date`
- Checked once a day, from Scryfall

## Activity {#settings-activity}
- A square a day for six months, darker for more changes
- A row per update — `+12 ~40 −1`
- Tap a day — `New`, `Changed`, `Removed`

## Card scanner {#settings-scanner}

- The images the scanner knows cards by → {#scanner}

## Pack odds {#settings-pack-odds}
- `MTGJSON build`, `Pack odds`, `Pull rates`

## Prices {#settings-prices}
- `Prices to <date>`
