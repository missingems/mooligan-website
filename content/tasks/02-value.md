Prices, history and buy-back, so you never trade blind.

## Price a card by pointing your camera {#tip-scan-price} [screen]
No more typing names or hunting through listings mid-trade. Point your camera at the card and its prices come up with it.
1. Tap `Scan`.
2. Hold the card in the middle of the screen until it lifts up.
3. Read its prices under it: regular, foil and etched.
4. Swipe sideways if yours is a different printing.
> Reprints with the same art look alike to the camera, so check the set under the card.

## See where a price has been {#tip-price-history} [screen]
Today's price is only half the story. See whether a card is climbing, crashing or holding steady over the last three months before you buy, sell or trade.
1. Open a card and scroll to `Market Price`.
2. Hold the chart for a moment.
3. Slide along it: the date and each finish's price that day appear above.
> A quick swipe across the chart moves to the next card; hold first to read it.

## Spot what a new set did to a price {#tip-set-markers} [screen]
Reprints and new combos move prices overnight. The chart marks the days new sets came out, so you can see what happened to a card when they did.
1. Open a card's `Market Price` chart.
2. Look for the set symbols along the top: each is a release day.
3. Hold and slide to that day to compare before and after.

## Know the foil premium {#tip-foil} [screen]
Is the shiny one worth it? Every finish is priced and charted on its own.
1. Open a card and scroll to `Market Price`.
2. Compare the capsules under the chart: `Regular`, `Foil`, `Etched`.
3. Each finish is its own line on the chart: Regular black, Foil orange, Etched purple.

## Find out what a shop will pay {#tip-buy-back} [screen]
Selling cards? See what a vendor pays before you walk up to the counter, and how much of its own price that is.
1. Open a card and scroll to `Market Price`.
2. Tap `Buy Back`.
3. Read Card Kingdom's buy price for each finish, and its share of retail.
4. Tap `Sell to Card Kingdom` to go to its buylist.

## Buy the single you need {#tip-buy} [screen]
Found the card you're missing? Go straight to a listing.
1. Open the card and scroll to `Market Price`.
2. Tap the price of the finish you want.
3. The card opens on TCGplayer.

## Find a set's most valuable cards {#tip-set-value} [screen]
Just opened a box, or about to? Put a set's priciest cards at the top.
1. Tap `Browse`, then the set.
2. Tap the order button, `↑ Name`.
3. Tap `Price`, then `Descending`.
