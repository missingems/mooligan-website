## Open a tile
1. Tap a tile. ![](figure:tiles-tap)
2. Swipe for the next. ![](figure:insight-swipe)
3. Pull down to close. ![](figure:insight-close)

## Set & rarity
1. Read the symbol’s colour. ![](figure:tile-rarity)
2. Tap `View` for the set. ![](figure:view-set)

> Silver is uncommon, gold rare, orange-red mythic.

## Collector number
1. Tap the number. ![](figure:tile-number)

> Borderless, showcase and extended-art printings are named here.

## Colour identity
1. Tap the colours. ![](figure:tile-identity)
2. Tap a lit spot for its colour. ![](figure:identity-lit)

> A basic land type counts, printed symbol or not.

## Mana value
1. Tap the mana value. ![](figure:tile-mana)
2. Count each symbol. ![](figure:mana-count)

> X counts as nothing, except on the stack.

## Loyalty
1. Tap a planeswalker’s loyalty. ![](figure:tile-loyalty)

> Its starting loyalty, and what each ability adds or takes away.

## Power & toughness
1. Tap a creature’s power and toughness. ![](figure:tile-pt)

> The damage it deals, and the damage it takes to destroy it.
