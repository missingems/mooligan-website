## Release dates
1. Scroll the `Sets` list. ![](figure:sets-dates)

> Newest first. Each date stays at the top while you scroll its sets.

## Upcoming sets
1. Look for the hourglass. ![](figure:sets-upcoming)

> Sets still to come sit at the top.

## Set codes
1. Read the code under the name. ![](figure:sets-code)

> Beside it, how many cards the set has.

## Commander decks and extras
1. Find them boxed with their set. ![](figure:sets-family)

> Commander decks, tokens and promos sit with the set they came out with.

## Search
1. Tap search at the bottom. ![](figure:sets-search)
2. Type part of a set’s name. ![](figure:sets-search-type)

> Names only, not codes. `Cancel` brings every set back.

## Refresh
1. Pull the list down. ![](figure:sets-refresh)

> The list is kept on your phone, so it opens offline.
