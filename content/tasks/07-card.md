Everything about one card: its text, what it means, where it's legal, how rare it is, what it's worth, and its other printings.

## Opening and closing {#card-open} [screen]
Where: tap a card anywhere — in a set, in Search, after a scan, in a pack → {#set-grid}, {#search-all}, {#scan-result}, {#pack-reveal}
- It zooms out of the card you tapped and covers the tabs
- ✕ at the top left, or swipe down — close it, back to where you were
- From a scan or a pack — Back at the top left

## Card after card {#card-pager}
- Swipe sideways — the next or previous card, in the order they were listed, filters and all
- Opened from a set — every card in it; from Search — every card found; from a scan — the card's printings; from a pack — the pack's cards

### The strip {#card-strip}
The row of small cards at the bottom of the screen.
- One small card for each card you can swipe to; the one on show sits under the lens
- Tap a small card — jump to it
- Hold and slide along — a card floats up showing where you are, with a tick for each card you pass; let go to land on it
- Flick it — it coasts, and lands where it stops

### Add to Collection {#card-add}
- `+` beside the strip — coming soon → {#collection}

## The card {#card-image}
- Tilt your phone — the card leans and catches the light
- A shimmer — this printing was made only in foil
- Round arrow on the card's edge — it has another face; tap to turn it over → {#symbols-turn}
- Turned over — the text, tiles and background show that face

## Name, cost and text {#card-text}
- Name, and its mana cost in symbols → {#symbols-mana}
- Type line — e.g. Creature — Goblin
- Rules text — with mana and tap symbols in place
- Underlined words — keywords, e.g. Flying
- Faded italics in brackets — reminder text, explaining a keyword
- Grey italics — flavour text
- Split and adventure cards — both halves side by side

## Information {#card-information}
A row of tiles under the card; scroll it sideways. Each tile opens a page explaining it.
- Tap a tile — its page fills the screen
- Swipe sideways — the next tile's page; or tap its badge at the top
- ✕, or pull the page down — close it

### Set & rarity {#insight-set}
- The tile — the set's symbol and code, coloured by rarity; the rarity under it → {#symbols-rarity}
- The page — which set the card is from, and how rare it is
- Rarities — how often a Play Booster has one, whatever the card: common and uncommon every pack, rare 1 in 1.2, mythic rare 1 in 7
- `Set`, `Code`, `Released`, `Set type` — e.g. Expansion
- `Printing` — `First printing` or `Reprint`
- `View …` — the set's cards → {#set-page}

### Collector number {#insight-collector}
- The tile — e.g. `#289`
- The page — which printing this is: versions with another frame, border or art get numbers of their own, usually after the main set
- `Number`, `Set`, `Cards in set`
- `Treatment` — for special versions: Borderless, Showcase, Extended Art, Full Art, Etched, Serialized

### Colour identity {#insight-identity}
- The tile — the symbols of the card's colours; {C} for colourless
- The page — the card, dimmed but for lit spots on everything that gives it colour: mana symbols in its cost and text, a colour indicator, basic land types
- Tap a lit spot — its colour
- A two-faced card — tap a face to bring it forward
- One row per colour
- `Not printed as a symbol` — a land type such as Swamp counts as its colour
- Why it matters — in Commander, every card must fit your commander's colour identity

### Mana value {#insight-mana}
- The tile — the total cost
- The page — each symbol in the cost counted out: a number for itself, each other symbol for one
- X — counts as nothing, except while the spell is being cast

### Loyalty {#insight-loyalty}
Planeswalkers only.
- The tile — the starting loyalty
- `Starting loyalty` — the counters it enters with
- `Abilities` — what each one adds or takes away, e.g. +1, −3, −8
- One loyalty ability a turn, when you could cast a sorcery

### Power & toughness {#insight-pt}
Creatures only.
- The tile — e.g. 2/3
- `Power` — the damage it deals in combat
- `Toughness` — the damage it takes to destroy it in one turn
- `In combat` — both, as damage; damage wears off at the end of each turn
- A star or an X — the number is set by its rules text

## Turn over {#card-turn-over}
Under the tiles, on cards with two faces.
- `Flip` — flip cards turn upside down
- `Transform` — transforming cards turn to their back
- `Turn Over` — modal double-faced and other two-sided cards
- The same as the round arrow on the card

## Legality {#card-legality}
- Under the heading — this printing's release date
- Ten formats: Standard, Historic, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Pauper, Vintage, Penny, Commander, Brawl
- `Legal` — green: allowed in that format
- `Not Legal` — grey: not allowed
- `Banned` — red: banned
- `Restricted` — blue: one copy only

## Pull Rate {#card-pull-rate}
How many packs, on average, until you open this exact printing.
- `Available in 2 Products` — how many boosters hold it
- A card for each booster — e.g. `Play Booster`, `Collector Booster`; a booster of another set says that set's name too
- `Regular` and `Foil` — the odds for each finish; `Any Finish` when they aren't split
- `1 in 168 packs` — on average; `Every pack` — always there; big numbers in words, e.g. `1 in 2.4 million`
- A booster's picture, and `Open Pack` — tap to open one → {#pack-opening}
- Not there for cards never sold in packs

## Market Price {#card-market-price}
What the card sells for in US dollars, from TCGplayer.

### The chart {#price-chart}
- `Past 3 Months` — how far back it goes; up to 90 days
- A line for each finish — Regular in black (white in dark mode), Foil orange, Etched purple → {#symbols-finish}
- Set symbols along the top — the days new sets came out
- Hold a moment, then slide — the date and each price on that day; lift to hide
- A quick swipe across — the next card, not the chart

### Today's prices {#price-today}
- A capsule for each finish — the latest price
- `—` — no price for that finish
- Tap a price — the card on TCGplayer, to buy

### Buy Back {#buy-back}
- `Buy Back` — what a store pays for a copy, as a share of its own price, e.g. `50%–70%`
- Tap it — `Source`: `Card Kingdom`; each finish's buy price and share
- `Sell to Card Kingdom` — its buylist, in your browser
- ✕ — close

### If prices won't load {#price-failed}
- `No data available` — no price history for this card
- `Couldn't load prices` — tap `Retry`

## Prints and related cards {#card-prints}
Sideways rows; each card shows its set and prices.
- `Prints` — every other paper printing of the card, with how many there are
- `Tokens` — tokens the card makes
- `Combo Pieces` — cards it combos with
- `Meld Pieces` and `Meld Result` — cards that meld together, and what they become
- A row only shows when the card has any

## Related {#card-related}
- `Artist` — who painted it
- `Rulings` — the official rulings → {#card-rulings}

### Rulings {#card-rulings}
- Each ruling with its date
- `No Results for "…"` — the card has none
- `Couldn't Load Rulings` — tap `Retry`
- `Done` — close
