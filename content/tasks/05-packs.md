The thrill of cracking packs, with the real odds, for free.

## Open packs without the price tag {#tip-free-packs} [screen]
Try a set before you buy a box. Packs are rolled from each booster's real odds, and every card shows what it's worth.
1. Open a set in `Browse` and tap its name.
2. Tap `Open Play Booster`, or `Open Collector Booster`.
3. Drag across the pack from right to left.
4. Flick each card away to reveal the next.

## Know your odds before you buy {#tip-pull-rate} [screen]
Chasing one card? See how many packs it takes, on average, to open it.
1. Open the card and scroll to `Pull Rate`.
2. Read `1 in 168 packs` for each booster that holds it, regular and foil.
3. Tap `Open Pack` to try your luck.

## Play Booster or Collector Booster? {#tip-which-booster} [screen]
Some printings are far easier to find in one booster than another. Compare them side by side.
1. Open the printing you want, and scroll to `Pull Rate`.
2. Swipe through the boosters that hold it.
3. Compare their odds.

## Save the best for last {#tip-reveal} [screen]
Cards come out like a real pack: the commons first, the best card last. A strong buzz means a hit.
- Under each card — its rarity, price and how rare it is
- Back arrow — put the last card back
- `Reveal All` — straight to the whole pack

## Keep a running tally {#tip-tally} [screen]
Opened a few? See what they'd add up to.
1. Finish a pack.
2. Tap `Open Another`, or pull up the next pack.
3. After two, the tally shows packs, cards and total value.
