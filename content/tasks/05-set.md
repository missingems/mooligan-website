The cards of one set, with its details, filters and packs.

## The set's page {#set-screen} [screen]
Where: tap a set in Browse or in Search, or `View` the set from a card's Set & rarity page → {#sets-list}, {#search-results}, {#insight-set}
- At the top — the set's symbol and name; tap for its details → {#set-info}
- Under it — three buttons for colour, card type and order → {#set-filters}
- The cards, two to a row → {#set-grid}
- Back — to the sets
- Pull the cards down — reload them

## Set details {#set-info}
Where: tap the set's name at the top of its page
- `Set Symbol` — the set's mark
- `Set Code` — e.g. `TDM`
- `Release Date` — the day it came out, year first, e.g. `2025-04-11`
- `Number of Cards` — e.g. `286`
- `Open Play Booster`, `Open Draft Booster`, `Open Collector Booster` — open a pack of this set → {#pack-opening}

### Which packs a set has {#set-packs}
- Play Boosters — sets from 2024 on
- Draft Boosters — sets before 2024
- Collector Boosters — sets from 2019 on
- Only sets sold in packs: main sets, core sets, Masters sets and draft sets
- None for Commander decks, online-only sets, small sets, or sets not out yet

## Filters {#set-filters}
Three buttons above the cards. They stay in place as the cards scroll.
- Tap a button — its list drops down; pick options, and the cards change behind it
- Tap outside the list — close it
- A tick and a shaded row — what's picked
- The cards jump back to the top after each change

### Colour {#set-filter-colour}
- The button — shows the colours picked, or all six when none are
- `White`, `Blue`, `Black`, `Red`, `Green`, `Colorless` — tap each to switch it on or off
- One colour — that colour's cards only, e.g. Red alone shows mono-red cards
- Several colours — cards within them: White and Blue shows white, blue and white-blue cards
- `Colorless` — adds cards with no colour, such as many artifacts and lands
- Colour here means colour identity: mana symbols in a card's rules text count too → {#insight-identity}

### Card type {#set-filter-type}
- The button — shows the type picked, or `All`
- `All`, `Land`, `Artifact`, `Enchantment`, `Instant`, `Sorcery`, `Planeswalker`, `Creature`
- Pick several — cards of any of them, e.g. Creature and Artifact shows both
- `All` — clears the others

### Order {#set-filter-sort}
- The button — an arrow and the order, e.g. ↑ `Name`
- Sort by `Name`, `Price`, `Mana Value`, `Color`, `Rarity` or `Date`
- Then `Ascending` or `Descending`
- To begin with — `Name`, `Ascending`
- Most expensive first — `Price`, then `Descending`

## The cards {#set-grid}
- Two cards to a row; more load as you scroll down
- Tap a card — its page → {#card-page}
- A round arrow on a card's edge — it has two faces; tap the arrow to turn it over → {#symbols-turn}

## Searching a set {#set-search}
- Use Search: type `in:` and the set's code → {#search-in-set}

## If the cards won't load {#set-failed}
- A spinner — still loading
- `No results found.` — nothing matches the filters; change them
- `Failed to find "…"` — tap the `Try Again` button below it
- `Resolving...` — trying again
