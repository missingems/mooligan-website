Symbols, settings, and what to do when something's off.

## Symbols {#ref-symbols}
- Hourglass — a set not out yet
- Grey pill — a set's code, e.g. `TDM`
- Two arrows on a card's edge — turns to its back
- Curved arrow — a two-sided card: turn it over
- Arrow round a corner — a flip card: turn it upside down
- Rarity — black common, silver uncommon, gold rare, orange-red mythic
- Prices — Regular black, Foil orange, Etched purple
- Legality — green legal, grey not legal, red banned, blue restricted

## Settings {#ref-settings}
Nothing to set: Settings shows what's on your phone and how up to date it is.
- Green tick — up to date
- Orange — updating now
- Red cross — failed; it tries again later
- `Card catalog` — every card; checked once a day
- `Activity` — what changed, day by day → {#tip-changes}
- `Card scanner` — the cards the scanner can recognise
- `Pack odds` — the boosters packs are rolled from
- `Prices` — checked four times a day

## If something's off {#ref-help}
- Sets won't load — check your connection, then tap `Retry`
- A set's cards won't load — tap `Try Again`
- `No results found.` — the filters leave nothing; change them
- The camera stays black — iPhone Settings → Mooligan → Camera
- The scanner can't find a card — one card, all four corners in view, even light, hold still
- The scanner found another printing — swipe sideways to yours
- Prices won't load — tap `Retry`
- A pack won't open — tap `Try Again`
