Where: a tab to come, from `data.mooligan.com`
- One page per format: Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Pauper, Vintage
- Swipe to change the format
- Pick a time frame — 7, 14 or 30 days

## Latest meta {#explore-meta} [planned]
- The 16 most played archetypes in the time frame
- Each an archetype row → {#archetype-row}
- Tap an archetype → {#archetype}
- `View more` — every archetype of the format → {#metagame}
- The whole metagame → {#metagame}

## Latest event {#explore-event} [planned]
- The newest event with a winner, as an event row → {#event-row}
- Tap it → {#event}

## Upcoming sets {#explore-upcoming}
- Sets still to come, under an hourglass, with their dates → {#upcoming-sets}
- Tap one for its cards and details → {#set-page}

## Released sets {#explore-released}
- Every released set, newest first: date, symbol, code, name, number of cards → {#set-row}
- Tap one → {#set-page}
